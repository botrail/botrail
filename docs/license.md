Starting with version 0.6.0, botrail is source-available under two public
licenses. You may use botrail under either license whose conditions cover your
use. Use that is not covered by either license requires a separate written
commercial license from 株式会社UnRobotics.

This page is a practical summary, not a replacement for the license terms. If
this page and a license differ, the license terms control.

## Public license options

### Small businesses

The [PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/small-business/1.0.0)
permits use for the benefit of a company only when both of its size conditions
are met:

- fewer than 100 total employees and independent contractors; and
- less than USD 1,000,000 (2019) total revenue in the prior tax year, adjusted
  for inflation as specified by the license.

The license defines “your company” to include organizations under common
control. Read the full terms when counting people, revenue, and related
organizations.

### Noncommercial use

The [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0)
permits noncommercial purposes and the categories of personal and institutional
use described in its terms. A commercial evaluation or a project with an
anticipated commercial application is not automatically covered merely because
it is called research, testing, or a proof of concept.

These licenses impose field-of-use and organization-size conditions, so botrail
0.6.0 and later is source-available software, not open-source software under the
[Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd).

## Common questions

### Which license do I choose?

You do not need to make a repository change or send a notice to choose between
the two public licenses. Your actual use must satisfy all conditions of at least
one of them. If it does not, or if you are unsure, contact UnRobotics before
using botrail.

### Does an integrator's license cover its customer?

No license under the PolyForm terms can be sublicensed or transferred. Every
party that uses botrail must independently have rights for that use. If an
integrator delivers botrail itself, a runtime containing botrail, or a project
that requires the customer to run botrail, the customer's use must qualify
under a public license or be covered by a commercial agreement.

### Are generated files covered by the botrail license?

The license governs the botrail software. An ordinary generated file that does
not contain botrail code is not made subject to these terms merely because
botrail created it. A deliverable can still contain botrail software, modified
botrail code, or third-party material with its own terms, so inspect what you
distribute. This does not change any rights in your input data.

### Can a large organization evaluate botrail for free?

Only when the evaluation is permitted by the PolyForm Noncommercial terms or a
separate written agreement. A large organization's commercial proof of concept
is not granted a special evaluation exception by this repository.

### What happened to the MIT License?

Versions v0.5.0 and earlier remain under the MIT License included with those
releases. The 0.6.0 change does not revoke rights already granted for earlier
versions. New code and distributions must be assessed using the license
materials shipped with their version.

### Does a license mean a robot cell is safe or certified?

No. A botrail license does not provide functional-safety certification and does
not replace risk assessment, validation, guarding, controller safety functions,
or compliance with applicable laws and standards.
