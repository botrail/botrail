# Cover bolting cell

Run `python examples/assembly/cover_bolting_demo.py --studio`. The bench,
fixtures and equipment details are generated in Python by
`_cover_bolting_cell.py`; the tool references are generated by
`_cover_bolting_tooling.py`. No environment asset needs rebuilding when the
layout changes. `--catalog` also loads the assembly packs.

Product references were checked on 2026-09-12. The bench and E-stop use
manufacturer dimensions and product identities, with authored simplified
geometry rather than manufacturer CAD. Their source URLs are also in the BOM.

The workpiece display meshes retain the original collision envelopes, grasp
geometry, hole axes and engagement windows. Fine machining, internal gears,
electrical wiring and robot dress packs are omitted. Decorative covers and
hardware do not collide; the bench frame, fixtures and panel bracket do.
The generated workpiece visuals are included when saving a `.botrail` project.

## Why the wrist changed

The former MPH-2D was a custom demo bracket, not an orderable product. Its
108 mm boom carried an axial screwdriver envelope, putting the bit roughly
421 mm from the wrist axis at zero stroke. Neither the attachment strength
nor assembled CoG/inertia had been established.

The new stack uses the two sides of a commercial Dual QC, with the
screwdriver on the +X side and the gripper on the −X side of the reference
changer frame. Both remain installed throughout the cycle. The driver is
supported at its side, around the middle of its housing. With the 50 mm
extender, its simulated working bit is about 209 mm from the wrist axis at
zero process stroke, and 236 mm at full stroke. These are kinematic offsets,
not a load qualification or a claim about the whole hand's maximum width.

The reference mounting centers are derived from the [Quick Changers v2.0
mechanical drawing, page 8](https://onrobot.com/storage/datasheets/quick-changers/datasheet_quick_changers_v2.0_en.pdf).
The driver native nose datum (153, 0, 81) mm and its side attachment follow
the [OnRobot UR manual v1.3.2, sections 8.2–8.3](https://www.wmh-trans.co.uk/file.php?filename=ONR-103961%2FUser+Manual+For+UR+Robots+Quick+Changer+Screwdriver+v1.3.2.pdf).
RG6 grasp height is derived from a width gauge against the loaded finger
geometry, compensating for the difference between its fixed TCP and moving
pads. The regression check requires the pad centers to be 5–15 mm below the
30 mm boss top during the actual grip.

Body outlines, latch details, taper, screw carrier and collision shapes are
approximations; they do not establish detailed mating tolerances.
The [extender drawing](https://onrobot.com/storage/technical_drawings/bit-extenders.pdf)
specifies +50 mm reach and a 12.2 mm diameter. The process shank zero is
17 mm inside the nominal nose datum plus this extension; it is not the
manufacturer controller's home value. Screws on the extender are exposed,
so the unextended screwdriver's full-retraction feature is not represented.

## Connection and verification boundary

The Dual QC must use the Compute Box route, **not the UR wrist tool
connector**. This is stated in the [OnRobot UR manual v1.17.0, section
4.3](https://media.ecosphere-solutions.de/instructions/de/onrobot/RG2/User_Manual_For_UR_Robots_Quick_Changer_RG2_v1.17.0_EN.pdf).
The screwdriver needs 20–25 V, up to 4.5 A; the normal low-current QC v2 is
not suitable. v3 109878 is selected here; older v2 104293 is not relabeled as
v3. The RG6 and screwdriver are operated sequentially. The original demo
START/BUSY/OK/NOK state machine and numbered simulation I/O remain abstract;
they are not an implementation of OnRobot's URCap or a physical pinout.

The published masses sum to **4.76 kg with the 0.6 kg cover**, before
unverified accessory/cable/mounting contributions. UR5e is rated for 5 kg;
this small nominal margin does not prove the installed assembly is within
its load curve. Complete mounted mass, CoG, inertia, grip force/friction,
fasteners, cable routing/current capacity and installed URCap/Compute Box
versions need physical engineering/commissioning. Unknown accessory masses
are omitted from the BOM rather than filled with estimates. The simulated
collision and fastening checks do not qualify these hardware conditions.

Both normal and `--catalog` execution use this commercial tooling reference.
`--catalog` selects only the feeder, screws and workpiece packs; it no longer
switches back to the generic SD5-340 screwdriver.
